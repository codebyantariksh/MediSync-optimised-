How a shortage gets resolved

From a rising demand curve to stock on the shelf, in four steps.

  1. Read consumption

    Each facility's recent 14 days of use are read from its stock data.

  2. Flag surges

    A Support Vector Machine compares each curve with normal and outbreak patterns and shows a risk percentage.

  3. Match supply

    Medicines with less than five days of cover are matched to facilities holding more than they need, taking batches that expire first.

  4. Donor approves

    The donor reviews the request and authorizes the release. The transfer then appears on the dashboard.

Built for safe, fair distribution

Rules that protect donors, prioritise life-saving medicines and keep every stock change accountable.

Surge prediction

An RBF-kernel SVM scores every facility and medicine, so rising demand is visible before stock runs out.

Two ways to allocate

Choose urgency first or fair share. Each mode reports how many critical needs it meets and a fairness score (Jain's index).

Expiry-aware dispatch

Transfers take from the batches that expire first, and stock likely to expire unused is flagged. Donors always keep their own reserve.

Reviewed stock updates

Facilities submit stock changes, and the MediSync team approves them before they go live on the network.
